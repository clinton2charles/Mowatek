About Us

Engineering Excellence in Water Solutions Since 1983

Mowatek Nigeria Limited is a leading engineering consulting and contracting firm specializing in water and wastewater treatment, with over 20 years of proven service to the water industry. We deliver end-to-end engineering solutions across process, mechanical, electrical, instrumentation, and civil disciplines.

Our expertise extends beyond water into industrial oil field projects, utility engineering maintenance, and operations outsourcing. We combine technical depth with practical execution to deliver reliable, sustainable, and cost-effective solutions that drive long-term value for our clients.

We believe in building strong partnerships founded on integrity, technical excellence, and measurable results ensuring every project meets the highest standards of safety, quality, and performance.
