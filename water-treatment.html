About Water Treatment

Why Water Treatment Matters

Access to clean, safe water is fundamental to health, economic development, and environmental sustainability. Water treatment is the process of removing contaminants, pathogens, and impurities to make water safe for consumption, industrial use, and environmental discharge.

At Mowatek, we understand that every water source presents unique challenges. Our comprehensive water treatment solutions combine proven technologies with innovative approaches to deliver reliable, cost-effective, and sustainable water purification systems tailored to your specific needs.

Our Solutions

Comprehensive Water Treatment Technologies

State-of-the-art treatment systems designed for residential, commercial, and industrial applications.

Advanced Filtration

Multi-stage filtration systems including sand filters, carbon filters, and membrane filtration to remove suspended solids, sediments, and organic matter.

  • Sand & media filtration
  • Activated carbon systems
  • Ultrafiltration membranes

Reverse Osmosis

High-performance RO systems that remove up to 99% of dissolved salts, minerals, and contaminants for ultra-pure water production.

  • Desalination systems
  • High-purity water production
  • Industrial-grade RO units

UV Disinfection

Chemical-free ultraviolet disinfection systems that effectively eliminate bacteria, viruses, and pathogens without altering water chemistry.

  • UV-C sterilization
  • 99.9% pathogen removal
  • No chemical byproducts

Chemical Treatment

Precise dosing and chemical treatment processes for coagulation, pH adjustment, and water conditioning to meet specific quality requirements.

  • Coagulation & flocculation
  • pH control systems
  • Automated dosing pumps

Water Softening

Ion exchange and lime softening systems that remove hardness-causing minerals, protecting equipment and improving water quality.

  • Ion exchange resins
  • Lime softening plants
  • Scale prevention systems

Smart Monitoring

Real-time monitoring and automated control systems with IoT integration for optimal performance and early problem detection.

  • SCADA systems
  • Remote monitoring
  • Automated alerts
