About Wastewater Treatment

Why Wastewater Treatment Is Critical

Wastewater and sewage treatment is essential for protecting public health, preserving water resources, and maintaining environmental sustainability. Untreated wastewater poses serious risks to human health and ecosystems, making proper treatment not just a regulatory requirement, but a fundamental responsibility.

At Mowatek, we design and implement comprehensive wastewater treatment systems that efficiently remove pollutants, organic matter, and pathogens. Our solutions ensure compliance with environmental regulations while enabling safe water reuse and protecting receiving water bodies from contamination.

Our Solutions

Comprehensive Wastewater Treatment Technologies

Advanced treatment processes designed for municipal, industrial, and commercial wastewater management.

Primary Treatment

Physical and mechanical processes that remove large solids, grit, and settleable materials through screening, sedimentation, and flotation.

  • Bar screens & grit chambers
  • Sedimentation tanks
  • Oil & grease removal

Biological Treatment

Advanced biological processes using microorganisms to break down organic matter and nutrients through aerobic and anaerobic treatment methods.

  • Activated sludge systems
  • Moving bed biofilm reactors
  • Membrane bioreactors (MBR)

Tertiary Treatment

Advanced polishing processes for removing remaining suspended solids, nutrients, and pathogens to achieve high-quality effluent standards.

  • Sand filtration systems
  • Nutrient removal (N & P)
  • UV & chlorine disinfection

Sludge Management

Comprehensive sludge treatment and disposal solutions including thickening, digestion, dewatering, and beneficial reuse options.

  • Anaerobic digestion
  • Mechanical dewatering
  • Biosolids management

Industrial Systems

Specialized treatment for industrial wastewater with unique contaminants including heavy metals, chemicals, and high-strength organic loads.

  • Chemical treatment systems
  • Heavy metal removal
  • Advanced oxidation processes

Water Reclamation

Advanced treatment for water reuse applications including irrigation, industrial processes, and non-potable urban uses.

  • Membrane filtration
  • Reverse osmosis systems
  • Multi-barrier treatment
